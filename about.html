About

Luke Chase is an actor based in Los Angeles.

After earning his electrical engineering degree from Cal Poly, San Luis Obispo, he left engineering to pursue acting full time. Since moving to LA two years ago, he has performed in three 60-minute Shakespeare productions at the Hobgoblin Playhouse in Hollywood and worked on-camera with independent filmmakers and student directors.

He takes classes at Playhouse West in North Hollywood, which is a Meisner school. He is currently rehearsing for a production of Dog Sees God: Confessions of a Teenage Blockhead, coming January 2027, in which he’ll be playing CB.

Outside of acting, Luke is a lifelong surfer and a writer drawn to stories about freedom, justice, and our connection to the natural world.
